# Ten-minute device checklist

Run this on the phone the evening before you first rely on the build. It is ordered by risk: the things at the top are the ones a simulator cannot prove and a real iPhone can break. Each step says what "pass" looks like; anything else is a bug worth reporting with the step number.

Set-up (one minute): install the build, open it, finish onboarding and grant the alarm permission. In **Settings → Diagnostics** confirm *Alarm mechanism* says **AlarmKit**, *System alarms authorised* says **Yes**, *Volume control* says **Ready**, and *Bundled sounds* says **All present**. Create one alarm two minutes out with the **Shake** mission, *Override device volume* on, *Gradually increase volume* off. Turn the phone's volume down to about a quarter.

| # | Test | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Volume lock.** Lock the phone. When the system alert fires, tap **Start mission**. On the ring screen press volume-down three times, quickly. | The sound dips for a fraction of a second each time and comes straight back to full. The pill at the top of the screen flashes *Volume restored* on each press. No system volume HUD appears (expected). |
| 2 | **Volume lock during the mission.** Slide to start the mission. While shaking, press volume-down again. | Same as step 1: momentary dip, straight back, *Volume restored* flash. |
| 3 | **Swipe home while ringing.** Swipe up to the Home screen and wait. | The alarm keeps sounding at full volume. Within about five seconds an *Alarm still ringing* notification appears (silent, time-sensitive). Tapping it opens the app straight onto the ring/mission screen with the shake count where you left it. |
| 4 | **Force-quit from the app switcher.** With the alarm ringing in the app, open the app switcher and swipe SuperAlarm away. Start a stopwatch and do nothing. | The in-app sound stops (the process is gone). Within about 30 seconds a **Still asleep?** system alarm sounds. If you press Stop on it, another lands within a minute, and they keep coming. Tap **Start mission** on any of them, or open the app by hand: it opens directly onto the ring screen for the same alarm with the mission still owed, not the alarm list. |
| 5 | **Complete the mission after a kill.** Shake to the goal. | The alarm stops, the wake-up check (if enabled) follows, and no further *Still asleep?* alarm arrives in the next five minutes. Diagnostics → *Saved ring state* says **None**. |
| 6 | **Lock screen.** Set another alarm two minutes out, lock the phone, put it face down. | The system alert sounds through Silent mode with the alarm's tone (26.1+) and shows the label and a **Start mission** button. Pressing a volume button dismisses the system alert (Apple's behaviour) and a *Still asleep?* backstop lands within 30 seconds. |
| 7 | **Stop button and roll over.** On the lock-screen alert press **Stop** and leave the phone alone. | *Still asleep?* backstops arrive at roughly 30 s, 60 s, 2, 3, 5, 8 and 12 minutes. After the first one, *still ringing* notifications also appear roughly every 30 seconds. They stop only once you open the app and finish the mission. |
| 8 | **Walk mission.** Set an alarm with **Walk**, goal 30, fire it with *Test this alarm now*, stand up holding the phone and walk. | The hint turns green with *Movement detected — counting…* within a second of moving. The counter jumps in batches (that is the pedometer) and reaches 30 within roughly 35 steps. It never goes backwards. If Motion & Fitness is off, the screen says so with the exact Settings path instead of sitting at 0. |
| 9 | **Camera missions.** Push-up (camera) and Squat (camera): prop the phone, do three slow reps each. QR/Barcode: register a code, then scan it. Object scan: register something across the room, then photograph it in the same light. | Reps count on the down-then-up cycle, half-reps do not count, the on-screen gauge and counter read from arm's length. Barcode matches on the first steady frame. Object scan fills the similarity meter and passes; in different light it does not, and the *Can't complete this?* escape hatch appears after the configured delay. |
| 10 | **Cognitive missions and the escape hatch.** Math (Hard, 2 rounds), Memory, Typing; then on any mission wait for *Can't complete this?*, hold the button and type *I give up*. | Wrong answers shake and reset, right ones advance the round counter; the escape hatch requires both the phrase and a three-second hold, then stops the alarm and records a failure in Stats. |
| 11 | **Face ID mission.** | Face ID prompt appears; a successful scan completes the mission; cancelling returns to the mission screen with a retry button, never a dead end. |
| 12 | **Wake-up check.** Enable it on an alarm with a 1-minute delay, dismiss the alarm, lock the phone. | A minute later the *Are you sure you're awake?* notification arrives; opening it shows the countdown ring; ignoring it re-rings the alarm at the end of the window. |
| 13 | **Snooze.** Snooze once with a 1-minute interval, then kill the app from the switcher. | The snooze alarm still sounds a minute later as a system alert; opening the app lands on the ring screen with *Snoozed 1×*. |
| 14 | **Phone call.** Ring an alarm, receive a call, hang up. | The alarm resumes at full volume after the call. |
| 15 | **Dark Mode and large text.** Settings → Appearance: Dark and Light. iOS Settings → Accessibility → Larger Text at the largest setting. | Every screen readable in both modes; the ring screen, mission screens and settings rows reflow without clipped labels or overlapping controls. |

If step 4 or 7 fails, note the exact timings; those are the two behaviours that depend on the system rather than the app.
